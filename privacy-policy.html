01

Who We Are

PinkX Studio ("we", "us", or "our") is a world-class creative design agency based in Bangladesh, serving brands, startups, and enterprises globally. We provide A–Z brand identity, UI/UX design, web design, motion graphics, video production, and digital marketing creative services.

We are fully committed to protecting your personal information and your fundamental right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share your personal data when you use our website, request our services, or communicate with us in any way.

By using our website or engaging with our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have any questions, please contact us at hello@pinkx.studio — we always respond with care.

02

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is genuinely necessary to provide you with our services or improve your experience on our website. We operate on a minimum necessary data principle.

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact form submissions — your first name, last name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, selected service type, budget range, and project details you share with us.
  • Meeting bookings — scheduling data processed through Cal.com, including your name, email address, and chosen time slot for a discovery call.
  • Proposal requests — service configurations from our pricing estimator, estimated budgets, and any additional notes you choose to share.
  • WhatsApp communications — if you contact us via WhatsApp, the content of those messages and your contact details are accessible to us through the WhatsApp Business platform.

Information collected automatically:

  • Technical data — anonymised IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring website — collected purely for site improvement and security.

Important: We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial data, government IDs, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, health data, or biometric identifiers at any time.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for specific, legitimate business purposes only. We will never use your data in ways that are inconsistent with what is described in this policy.

  • To respond to enquiries — understanding your project needs and providing accurate, personalised quotes and proposals.
  • To deliver our services — communicating throughout the design and production process, sharing deliverables, and providing revisions.
  • To schedule and conduct meetings — confirming, rescheduling, or following up on discovery calls and project meetings.
  • To improve our website — analysing anonymised usage patterns to make our site faster, more useful, and more accessible.
  • To comply with legal obligations — maintaining records as required by applicable laws, including financial and tax regulations in Bangladesh.
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud — detecting and preventing abuse, unauthorized access, or other illegal activities.

We never sell, rent, barter, or trade your personal information to third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose. Your data serves only you.

04

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your information with a very limited number of trusted service providers who help us operate our business. All such parties are carefully vetted and are contractually required to handle your data with the same level of care we do.

  • Cal.com — Used for scheduling discovery calls. When you book a meeting, your name and email are shared with Cal.com to generate a calendar invitation. Governed by cal.com/privacy.
  • Google Workspace — We use Gmail and Google Drive for internal communication and project file management. Google's data processing practices are governed by their Data Processing Addendum.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — If you contact us via WhatsApp, your communications are subject to Meta's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We recommend using email if you prefer minimal data sharing.
  • Website hosting & CDN providers — Our infrastructure providers may process technical data (e.g., IP addresses) in the course of delivering web pages securely and reliably.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, social media pixels, affiliate tracking, or any cross-site tracking technologies. We will never share your data with data brokers.

We may disclose personal information if required by applicable law, court order, or legal process, or if we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of PinkX Studio, our clients, or the public.

05

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Our general retention guidelines are:

  • Project and communication data — retained for up to 3 years after the completion or last activity related to a project, to allow for warranty support, future collaboration, and legal compliance.
  • Website analytics data — retained in anonymised, aggregated form for up to 24 months before being permanently deleted.
  • Enquiries that did not result in a project — contact form submissions and proposals are deleted within 12 months if no further engagement occurs.
  • Financial and invoicing records — retained for the period required by Bangladesh financial regulations (typically 7 years).

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@pinkx.studio. We will process your request within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

06

Your Rights

We believe in giving you genuine control over your personal data. Depending on your location and applicable laws (including GDPR for EU residents), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, free of charge, within 30 days.
  • Right to Rectification — Request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure — Request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Restriction — Request that we temporarily stop processing your data while a dispute or investigation is ongoing.
  • Right to Object — Object to our processing of your data for specific purposes, including direct communications.
  • Right to Data Portability — Receive a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV) to transfer to another service.
  • Right Against Automated Decision-Making — We do not use automated profiling or decision-making that produces legal or significant effects on individuals.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@pinkx.studio with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request". We will verify your identity and respond within 30 days. There is no charge for exercising these rights.

07

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses a minimal, privacy-first approach to cookies and tracking. We are committed to not surveilling our visitors.

  • Strictly necessary cookies — Small, session-based cookies required for basic website functionality (e.g., remembering form state). These cannot be disabled.
  • Anonymised analytics — We may use a privacy-respecting analytics tool to understand aggregate traffic patterns. All data is anonymised — no personal data leaves your browser.

What we do NOT use: Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Google Ads tracking, Twitter/X tracking, LinkedIn Insight Tag, TikTok Pixel, or any cross-site advertising trackers. Your browsing activity stays private.

You can disable cookies entirely via your browser settings. For detailed instructions, visit aboutcookies.org.

08

Security

Protecting your data is a genuine priority for us, not just a checkbox. We implement multiple layers of security:

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) — All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.2+ protocols.
  • Access controls — Personal data is accessible only to authorised team members. We use strong password policies and two-factor authentication on all accounts.
  • Regular reviews — We periodically review our data handling practices, third-party relationships, and security measures to ensure ongoing compliance.
  • Minimal data storage — We only store what we need, and we regularly purge outdated records according to our retention policy above.

Despite our best efforts, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and applicable authorities as required by law.

Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at hello@pinkx.studio. We will delete such information promptly upon verification.
