Fair & Transparent
Our refund terms are designed to be clear and fair for both clients and our team.

01

Overview

At PinkX Studio, we are committed to delivering exceptional creative work. We understand that sometimes plans change, and we aim to handle all refund and cancellation requests with fairness, transparency, and professionalism.

This policy applies to all services offered by PinkX Studio, including but not limited to logo design, brand identity, UI/UX design, website design, motion graphics, video editing, packaging design, presentation design, social media design, and marketing creatives.

Our commitment: We will always try to resolve any dissatisfaction through revisions and open communication before any refund request is considered. Client satisfaction is our primary goal.

02

Deposit Policy

All projects require a 50% upfront deposit before work commences. This deposit covers the initial discovery, research, strategy, and design phase of your project.

  • Deposit (50%): Paid upfront to secure your project slot and commence work. This portion is non-refundable once design work has started, as it covers the time and resources invested in your project.
  • Final payment (50%): Due upon project completion and delivery of all agreed deliverables, before final files are released to the client.
  • Retainer plans: Monthly retainer fees are billed at the start of each month and are non-refundable once the month has commenced and work has begun.
03

Refund Eligibility

A full or partial refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Project not started: If you cancel before any work has commenced (within 24 hours of payment and before the discovery call or brief), a full refund of the deposit will be issued, minus any transaction fees.
  • PinkX Studio fails to deliver: If we are unable to deliver the agreed scope of work within a reasonable timeframe (defined as more than 2× the agreed deadline with no mutual extension agreed), a partial refund proportional to undelivered work will be issued.
  • Work deviates significantly from the agreed brief: If initial deliverables substantially fail to address the agreed creative brief, and cannot be resolved through the revision process, we will work with you to find a fair resolution, which may include partial credit or refund at our discretion.
04

Non-Refundable Services

The following are non-refundable under all circumstances:

  • Completed and approved work: Once a deliverable has been approved (verbally or in writing) by the client, no refund is available for that deliverable.
  • Change of mind: Refunds will not be issued due to a change of mind, change of direction, or change of business strategy after work has commenced.
  • Rush/express service fees: Any surcharge paid for expedited delivery is non-refundable once work has been prioritised and commenced.
  • Work delayed by client: If a project is delayed due to slow feedback, lack of content/assets, or unresponsiveness from the client side for more than 14 days, the deposit is forfeited and the project slot is released.
  • Stock assets, fonts, or third-party licenses: Any costs incurred for stock images, licensed fonts, or third-party tools purchased specifically for your project are non-refundable.
05

Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your project at any time by notifying us in writing via email at hello@pinkx.studio.

  • Cancellation before first deliverable: 75% refund of deposit, retaining 25% as an administrative and time commitment fee.
  • Cancellation after first deliverable presented: No refund of deposit. We retain the full deposit to cover the design work and resources invested.
  • Cancellation mid-project (past 50% completion): Final payment (or a portion of it) may still be owed for work completed to date, at a rate agreed between both parties.
  • Monthly retainer cancellation: Monthly retainers can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. Work during the final 30-day period will be completed and billed normally.

Upon cancellation, all files and work-in-progress created up to the cancellation date remain the property of PinkX Studio until all outstanding invoices are settled. Ownership transfers to the client upon full payment.

06

Revision Policy

Before requesting a refund, we strongly encourage clients to utilise our revision process. Most creative disagreements can be resolved through constructive feedback and revision rounds.

  • Starter plan: 3 included revision rounds per deliverable.
  • Growth & Premium plans: Unlimited revisions until satisfaction is achieved.
  • Additional revision rounds beyond the included amount may be purchased at an hourly rate of $30/hr.

Revisions must be based on the original agreed brief. Requests that represent a significant change in direction, scope, or concept will be treated as new work and quoted separately.

07

Disputes

We take all client concerns seriously and aim to resolve any disputes amicably and professionally. If you are unhappy with any aspect of our work or service, please follow these steps:

  • Step 1 — Contact us directly: Email hello@pinkx.studio with a detailed description of your concern. We respond within 2 business days.
  • Step 2 — Mediation call: If the issue cannot be resolved via email, we will schedule a call to discuss and work toward a resolution together.
  • Step 3 — Formal resolution: In the unlikely event that a resolution cannot be reached, disputes are governed by the laws of Bangladesh. PinkX Studio reserves the right to determine the final outcome within the bounds of this policy.

If a refund is approved, it will be processed within 7–14 business days to the original payment method used. We are not responsible for delays caused by payment processors or banks.
