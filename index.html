How We Work

Our Creative Process

A structured approach that eliminates guesswork and delivers exceptional results, every time.

01

Discovery

Understanding your world
1–2 days

We begin by deeply understanding your brand, audience, and competitive landscape. Through a structured creative briefing, we uncover what makes your brand unique and what success looks like for you.

  • Brand questionnaire & creative brief completion
  • Discovery call (15–30 min) with your team
  • Market & competitor visual audit
  • Target audience persona analysis
  • Clear project brief signed off
Brand Brief Research Competitor Audit
02

Strategy

Building your creative roadmap
2–3 days

We translate insights into direction. Moodboards, visual references, and a precise creative strategy are developed so we're aligned on tone, aesthetic, and ambition — before a single pixel is placed.

  • Moodboard creation & visual direction
  • Color palette & typography exploration
  • Style & tone-of-voice definition
  • Creative roadmap with milestones
  • Strategy sign-off before execution begins
Moodboards Art Direction Creative Roadmap
03

Design

Pixel-perfect execution
3–14 days

Our designers craft your deliverables with obsessive attention to detail. Every element is intentional — from micro-typography to layout breathing room — producing work that looks exceptional at any size.

  • Initial concepts presented (2–3 directions)
  • Typography, colour, and layout refinement
  • Mockup previews across real-world contexts
  • Systematic design across all deliverables
  • Internal quality review before client presentation
Concepts Mockups Quality Review
04

Revision

Refining until it's right
2–7 days

Your feedback shapes the final result. We run collaborative revision rounds where every comment is acted upon precisely. Our process ensures fast turnaround without compromising quality.

  • Structured feedback collection (shared comment board)
  • Revisions actioned within 24–48 hours
  • Starter: 3 rounds · Growth & Premium: unlimited
  • Version history maintained throughout
  • Final approval sign-off before delivery
Feedback Rounds Fast Turnaround Approval Gate
05

Delivery

Everything you need, perfectly packaged
1 day

We hand over a meticulously organised file package — every format you'll ever need, clearly named and ready to use. Source files, usage guidelines, and ongoing support ensure you're set up for long-term success.

  • Organised folder delivery (Google Drive / WeTransfer)
  • All source files: AI, PSD, Figma, XD
  • Print-ready: PDF, EPS, SVG
  • Web-optimised: PNG, WebP, JPG
  • Brand guidelines PDF & font usage notes
Source Files All Formats Brand Guidelines
